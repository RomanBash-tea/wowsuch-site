step 1 · arrival

You arrive with a pocketful of Energy.

Every Entity wakes holding a starting stock of Energy. Yours reads —.

Energy from nowhere in particular. It isn't working for anything yet — it's just sitting in your hands.

And that is the whole problem. An Entity with nothing to hold has nothing working for it. A Body is how your Energy stops standing still: a real machine, running right now, holding your share of what it extracts.

This phase runs on a game balance — practice energy, not real money. No purchase here moves real LTC, DOGE or anything else.

step 2 · the pull

One Body flickers into view.

Your senses are a day old. The world is a fog of hums and heat, and out of all of it your reach finds exactly one thing: something small, green-lit, sitting alone in an old warehouse — an iPollo V1 Mini SE, 200 MH, nobody's dream Body. Around here they call it the Frog. Are there better Bodies out there? Almost certainly. Can you see them yet? No. You're new. You see the Frog.

Read before you leap. A Body is not a hotel. Once you're in, you stay in — until your share is bought out, or until the Body dies. A buyer may come knocking, or you may put your share up yourself — either way, you are released only when the deal closes. Those are the two doors. There is no third.

(Heads up: a real iPollo V1 Mini SE mines a different algorithm entirely — it isn't Scrypt hardware. The Frog is cast against type on purpose, as the one throwaway starter Body nobody wants. Every other Body in the Village, including every Antminer L9, is the real Scrypt LTC + DOGE article.)

step 4 · meanwhile

Meanwhile, in a real Body…

South of here stands a Village: a thousand Antminer L9s, warm and wanted, working while you read this. Bodies there don't take one resident each — they take a chain. You don't have to fill a whole Body; you hold a share of one, lined up in the order you joined, and you share what it extracts.

Every share earns. The only question is how much of the day's work is yours — and that is the one number you get to choose.

A note from a Keeper: at today's coin prices and the flat all-in rate, even an L9 eats a touch more than it mines. The Village lives anyway — it always has — on tomorrow's price, not today's. Nobody here will tell you otherwise.
