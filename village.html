step 1 · arrival

You arrive with a pocketful of Energy.

Every Entity wakes holding a starting stock of Energy. Yours reads —.

Energy from nowhere in particular. It isn't working for anything yet — it's just sitting in your hands.

And that is the whole problem. An Entity with nothing to hold has nothing working for it. A Body is how your Energy stops standing still: a real machine, running right now, holding your share of what it extracts.

This phase runs on a game balance — practice energy, not real money. No purchase here moves real LTC, DOGE or anything else.

step 2 · the pull

One Body flickers into view.

Your senses are a day old. Around here they call it the Frog. Are there better Bodies out there? Almost certainly. Can you see them yet? No. You're new. You see the Frog.

This is where you start, not where you stay. The Frog is a dead end by construction. It isn't having a bad week and it isn't waiting for a better one — a 200 MH machine alone in an old warehouse is the best it will ever be, and next year it will be the same machine doing the same arithmetic. Nothing you learn, and nothing you do for it, moves that number.

step 4 · meanwhile

Meanwhile, in a real Body…

South of here stands a Village: a thousand Antminer L9s, warm and wanted, working while you read this. Bodies there don't take one resident each — they take a chain. You don't have to fill a whole Body; you hold a share of one, lined up in the order you joined, and you share what it extracts.

A share doesn't promise a profit. It gives you a fixed slice of whatever the Body extracts that day — and on a bad day, of what it costs. How big that slice is, is the one number you get to choose.

A note from a Keeper: at today's coin prices and the flat all-in rate, even an L9 eats a touch more than it mines. The Village lives anyway — it always has — on tomorrow's price, not today's. Nobody here will tell you otherwise.

dormant

Your Energy ran out.

You held no Body, so nothing was working for you, and the clock did what clocks do. The Frog was never going to fix that — on its very best day it was a 200 MH machine alone in an old warehouse.

Your Entity is dormant, not gone. Your Rune still stands in the ledger and the Village is still yours to walk: open any Body, read what it did today, take as long as you like. You simply cannot take a share of one until there is Energy in your hands again.

There is one way to get it, and this is it.

step 1 · your own numbers

Read what you are holding.

Open your Energy line: what you have, what it loses a day, and how many days that leaves you. It is three numbers and it takes ten seconds — and it is the only part of this the Frog cannot argue with.

step 2 · a real Body

Open one real Body.

Walk the Village, pick any Body that catches your eye, and open its telemetry: hashrate, temperature, what it actually did today. Nearly every Body down there is bound to a live machine, so what you are reading moved since yesterday. It is not a brochure.

step 3 · side by side

Now put the two next to each other.

On one side, a machine that will never be anything but what it is — you have seen its whole arithmetic, and that is all of it, for good. On the other, a real Body: some days up, some days down, and every one of those days on the screen in front of you. That is the difference worth choosing between. Pick where your Energy sits.

    Village Codex

    What is happening here, in plain terms:

    • Entityyou, before you've claimed anything to hold: a name in the ledger and a spark of your own.
    • Bodya real machine, working right now, holding your share of what it extracts.
    • Chainthe order of residents sharing one Body, lined up in the sequence they joined.
    • Keepera resident who watches a Body's real telemetry and earns extra for the duty.
    • Energywhat a Body extracts, settled nightly across the residents in the chain. Some nights a Body spends more energy than it extracts, and the settlement runs the other way.
    • Nightly payoutthe night’s settlement lands once a day; check back each morning. It can be a credit or a debit, depending on what the Body did that night.
