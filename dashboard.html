Village Codex

What is happening here, in plain terms:

  • Entityyou, before you've claimed anything to hold: a name in the ledger and a spark of your own.
  • Bodya real machine, working right now, holding your share of what it extracts.
  • Chainthe order of residents sharing one Body, lined up in the sequence they joined.
  • Keepera resident who watches a Body's real telemetry and earns extra for the duty.
  • Energywhat a Body extracts, settled nightly across the residents in the chain. Some nights a Body spends more energy than it extracts, and the settlement runs the other way.
  • Nightly payoutthe night’s settlement lands once a day; check back each morning. It can be a credit or a debit, depending on what the Body did that night.

This phase runs on a game balance only — practice energy, not real money.
